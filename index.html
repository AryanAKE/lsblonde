Our Story

A Sanctuary of
Style & Self‑Expression

Nestled on Guwahati Shillong Road at Six Mile, LS Blonde Unisex Salon was born from a simple belief — that every person deserves to feel extraordinary. We are more than a salon; we're a place where artistry meets care.

From a precision haircut to a full bridal transformation, our experienced team brings passion and expertise to every chair. We welcome everyone — because beauty truly has no boundaries.
